NOVA TRADE LLC - OFFICE IT RULES

Expected result against the GDPR example excerpt: LOW (about 0-20 %).
This document is a test file for the CyberLaw compliance checker. It is a typical office IT
policy that barely touches data protection.

1. Purpose
These rules explain how employees of Nova Trade LLC use company computers, phones and the
internet. They apply to all staff and interns.

2. Passwords
Every employee has a personal account. Passwords must have at least 8 characters and must not
be shared with colleagues. Passwords are changed every six months.

3. Computers and screens
Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Do not install games or unlicensed software.
Antivirus software is installed on every computer and must not be switched off.

4. USB drives and printing
Personal USB drives may not be connected to company computers. Collect your printouts from the
printer immediately.

5. Internet and e-mail
The internet may be used for work and, in moderation, for personal purposes during breaks.
Do not open attachments from unknown senders. Chain letters and advertising must not be
forwarded.

6. Customer files
Customer files are stored on the shared drive of the sales department. Staff should treat
customer information as confidential and not discuss it outside the company.

7. Lost or broken equipment
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT department on the same day so that
a replacement can be ordered.

8. Responsibility
The head of IT is responsible for these rules. Breaking the rules may lead to disciplinary
measures.
