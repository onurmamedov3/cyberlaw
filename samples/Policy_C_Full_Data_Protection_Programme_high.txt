NOVA TRADE LLC - DATA PROTECTION PROGRAMME

Expected result against the GDPR example excerpt: HIGH (about 90-100 %).
This document is a test file for the CyberLaw compliance checker. It addresses every requirement
of the GDPR example excerpt explicitly.

1. Purpose and accountability
This programme sets out how Nova Trade LLC, as controller, complies with the data protection
principles and how it demonstrates compliance. The Data Protection Officer (DPO) keeps a
compliance file with this programme, the records of processing, DPIAs, the breach register and
training records. An internal audit checks compliance every year and reports to the board.

2. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency
Personal data is processed lawfully, fairly and transparently. Individuals are always told, in
clear and plain language, what we do with their data before or when it is collected.

3. Purpose limitation
Every processing activity has specified, explicit and legitimate purposes, recorded before the
processing starts. Data is never used for a new purpose that is incompatible with the original
one; a new purpose requires a documented compatibility assessment approved by the DPO.

4. Data minimisation
We collect only data that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary for the
purpose. Every new form and system field is reviewed by the DPO before it goes live.

5. Accuracy
Customers can update their data in their account at any time. Data owners review the accuracy of
their data sets every year, and inaccurate data is corrected or erased without delay.

6. Storage limitation and retention schedule
Personal data is kept no longer than necessary, according to our retention schedule: customer
and order records 6 years after the end of the contract (tax law), job applications 6 months
after the recruitment decision, CCTV recordings 30 days, newsletter data until consent is
withdrawn. Data is deleted or anonymised automatically at the end of the retention period.

7. Lawful basis
Before any processing starts, its lawful basis is recorded in the records of processing:
consent, performance of a contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public interest or
legitimate interests. For legitimate interests, a balancing test against the rights of the
individual is documented.

8. Consent
Where processing is based on consent, we keep proof of consent: who consented, when, how and
the exact wording shown. Individuals are told before consenting that they can withdraw consent at
any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it: one click on the link in every e-mail or
one switch in the account settings. Processing based on that consent stops within 48 hours.

9. Privacy notice
When we collect personal data we give individuals a privacy notice with: our identity and
contact details; the contact details of the DPO; the purposes and the legal basis of the
processing; the recipients of the data; any transfer to a third country and the safeguards used;
the storage period; the rights of access, rectification and erasure; the right to withdraw
consent; and the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority.

10. Rights of individuals: access, rectification and erasure
Individuals can ask for confirmation whether we process their data and receive access to it and a
free copy. Inaccurate data is rectified and incomplete data completed without undue delay. Data is
erased without undue delay when it is no longer necessary, when consent is withdrawn or when it
was processed unlawfully. Every request is answered without undue delay and in any event within
one month of receipt. If a request is complex, the period can be extended by two further months;
the individual is informed of the extension and the reasons within the first month. All requests
are logged in a request register.

11. Data protection by design and by default
Every new system, product or change goes through a privacy-by-design checklist approved by the
DPO, covering minimisation, pseudonymisation, access control and retention. By default, only the
personal data necessary for each specific purpose is processed: optional fields are off by
default and customer profiles are private by default. Analytics use pseudonymised identifiers.

12. Processors and sub-processors
We use only processors that provide sufficient guarantees, checked with a security questionnaire
before the contract. Every processor signs a written data processing agreement which requires
it to: process data only on our documented instructions; bind its staff to confidentiality; take
the security measures of section 14; not engage a sub-processor without our prior written
authorisation; assist us with requests from individuals and with personal data breaches; delete
or return all personal data at the end of the services; and provide all information needed to
demonstrate compliance, including audits and inspections.

13. Records of processing activities
The DPO maintains a written, electronic record of all processing activities. For each activity it
contains: our name and contact details and those of the DPO; the purposes; the categories of
individuals and of personal data; the categories of recipients; transfers to third countries; the
time limits for erasure; and a general description of the security measures. The record is
reviewed every quarter and is made available to the supervisory authority on request.

14. Security of processing
Security measures are based on a risk assessment and include: encryption of personal data at
rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher); pseudonymisation where possible; role-based
access with least privilege and multi-factor authentication; redundant servers and monitoring to
ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience of our systems; daily
encrypted backups, with restore tests every quarter so that availability and access to personal
data can be restored within 4 hours after an incident; and a process for regularly testing,
assessing and evaluating the effectiveness of the measures, with quarterly vulnerability scans
and a yearly external penetration test. Employees and contractors with access to personal data
sign confidentiality agreements, are trained every year and may process data only on our
instructions.

15. Personal data breaches
Every employee reports a suspected breach to the DPO immediately. The DPO assesses the risk and,
unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals, notifies the supervisory
authority without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after we become aware of it. The
notification describes the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of
individuals and records concerned, the contact details of the DPO, the likely consequences and
the measures taken. If notification takes longer than 72 hours, the reasons for the delay are
included. Our processors must notify us without undue delay, and at the latest within 24 hours.
Every breach, including those not notified, is documented in the breach register with its facts,
effects and the remedial action taken. When a breach is likely to result in a high risk to
individuals, we inform the affected individuals without undue delay, in clear and plain language.

16. Data protection impact assessment
Before any processing that is likely to result in a high risk, in particular profiling or other
systematic evaluation, large-scale processing of special categories of data, or large-scale
monitoring of publicly accessible areas, we carry out a data protection impact assessment
(DPIA). The DPIA describes the processing and its purposes, assesses its necessity and
proportionality and the risks to individuals, and sets out the measures to address the risks.
The DPO advises on every DPIA.

17. Data Protection Officer
Nova Trade LLC has designated a Data Protection Officer, who reports directly to the board and
acts independently. The DPO's contact details (dpo@novatrade.example) are published on our
website and have been communicated to the supervisory authority.

18. International transfers
Personal data is transferred to a third country only if the European Commission has adopted an
adequacy decision for it, or with appropriate safeguards: the standard data protection clauses
adopted by the Commission, together with a transfer impact assessment, and only where individuals
have enforceable rights and effective legal remedies. All transfers are listed in the records of
processing.

19. Training and review
All staff complete data protection training when they join and every year after. This programme
is reviewed every year by the DPO and approved by the board.
