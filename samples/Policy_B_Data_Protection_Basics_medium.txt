NOVA TRADE LLC - DATA PROTECTION BASICS

Expected result against the GDPR example excerpt: MEDIUM (about 40-60 %).
This document is a test file for the CyberLaw compliance checker. About a third of the
requirements are covered fully, a third only partly or vaguely, and a third are missing.

1. Purpose
This policy describes how Nova Trade LLC handles personal data of customers and employees.

2. Why we collect personal data
We collect personal data only for specified purposes that are written down before the data is
collected: delivering orders, invoicing, customer support and employment. Data collected for one
of these purposes is not used for other, unrelated purposes.

3. Collecting only what we need
Forms ask only for the data needed for the purpose. For an order we ask for name, delivery
address, e-mail and phone number; we do not ask for date of birth or ID numbers.

4. Legal basis
Every processing activity has a recorded legal basis before it starts: the customer's consent,
the performance of a contract, a legal obligation (for example tax law) or our legitimate
interests.

5. Consent
Where we rely on consent, for example for the newsletter, the consent is recorded together with
the date.

6. Privacy notice
Our privacy notice is shown on the website and on every form where personal data is collected.
It states who we are and how to contact us, the purposes and legal basis of the processing, who
receives the data, how long the data is kept, the rights of individuals (access, correction,
deletion, withdrawing consent) and the right to complain to the supervisory authority.

7. Requests from individuals
Individuals may ask for a copy of their personal data, for the correction of wrong data or for
the deletion of their data. Requests are answered within one month of receipt.

8. How long we keep data
Personal data is kept only as long as needed for business purposes and then deleted.

9. Security
We protect personal data with appropriate security: personal user accounts, passwords and
antivirus software. The file server is backed up every week.

10. Suppliers
We work only with reputable suppliers (hosting, payroll, delivery) and sign a contract with them.

11. Incidents
Security incidents involving personal data are reported to management, which decides whether
the authorities need to be informed.

12. Data outside the country
Personal data is not transferred to countries outside the European Union. If a transfer becomes
necessary, it will only take place with the standard data protection clauses adopted by the
European Commission.
